Proposed Website
Concept review

A website built to book jobs — not just to look good.

Every element exists to turn a visitor into a phone call — and to show the work, not just talk about it. The planet in the header is rendered live in the browser: no stock video, no plugins, nothing to load.

Built to convert

  • Tap-to-call bar pinned to the bottom of every phone screen
  • Estimate form that captures project type, timeline and budget before the first visit
  • Job photos in the header, so the work shows before a word is read

Built to be found

  • Structured data for Google — contractor profile plus FAQ rich results
  • Real text content, not an image of a website
  • Fast and mobile-first — what local search actually rewards

Built to last

  • One self-contained file. No page builder, no plugins, no subscription
  • Nothing to update, nothing to break, no renewal that doubles
  • You own it outright

Why it wins work

01

It answers objections before the call

A seven-question FAQ and a four-step process section handle what actually stalls a remodel decision: cost, timeline, mess and surprises.

02

It proves the work

Real project scope — beams, waterproofing, roof tie-ins — plus a cutaway of a shower wall and a drag-to-compare slider.

03

It gets remembered

Named after the largest planet in the solar system — so the site opens on one. Nobody else in this market looks like this.
